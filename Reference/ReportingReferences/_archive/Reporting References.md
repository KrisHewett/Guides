**Draft - for analytics, governance, and reporting orientation only**

## 1. Health Care Act 2008 (SA)

### Primary legislation
- Health Care Act 2008 (SA)  
  <https://www.legislation.sa.gov.au/lz?path=%2FC%2FA%2FHEALTH%20CARE%20ACT%202008>

### Key reporting obligations
- **Incident reporting:** Reporting of incidents that result in harm or potential harm, including sentinel and adverse events.
- **Performance reporting:** Reporting on system performance and health outcomes.
- **Licensing and accreditation:** Compliance with service authorisation and accreditation requirements.
- **System reporting:** Annual and four-yearly reporting on health system performance.

### Supporting reference
- SA Health Performance Framework  
  <https://www.sahealth.sa.gov.au/wps/wcm/connect/public+content/sa+health+internet/about+us/governance/sa+health+performance+framework>

## 4. Mental Health Act 2009 (SA)

### Primary legislation
- Mental Health Act 2009 (SA)  
  <https://www.legislation.sa.gov.au/lz?path=%2FC%2FA%2FMENTAL%20HEALTH%20ACT%202009>

### Reporting obligations
- Serious adverse events and critical incidents.
- Restraint and seclusion (including annual reporting).
- Electroconvulsive Treatment (ECT).
- Deaths in care.
- Sexual safety incidents.
- Mandatory reporting to the Chief Psychiatrist.

### Oversight authority
- Office of the Chief Psychiatrist - Policies, Standards and Reports  
  <https://www.chiefpsychiatrist.sa.gov.au/policies-standards-and-reports>
