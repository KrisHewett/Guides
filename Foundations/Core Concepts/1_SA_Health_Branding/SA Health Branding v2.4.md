## LOGO

The GOVERNMENT OF SOUTH AUSTRALIA LOGO

**Font:** Frutiger LT Std Bold

- Blue: #004B88
- Red: #ED174C
- Yellow: #FCAF17
- Charcoal Grey: #808285
- Black: #231F20

Use **official SA Health logo only from approved sources**.

- Maintain clear space and minimum size requirements:
  - **Digital:** 150px width
  - **Print:** 30mm width
- must be protected by an isolation zone of clear space at all times
- The clear space, width measuring 25% of the diameter of the roundel, must surround the logo on all sides
- It cannot be impinged upon by graphic elements, other logos, the edge of a page or advertisement
- It must be free of background colour changes

- Incorrect usage examples:
  - Stretching
  - Color changes
  - Adding shadows or effects
